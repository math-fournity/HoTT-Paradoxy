## 3. Ferreirós ——SEP「早期集合论」条目（全文在手）

- 本地副本：`搜索原始响应-20261017/sep-settheory-early-ferreiros.html/.txt`，sha256[:16]（html）= `b6b5504f229f1aaa`。
- 来源 URL：https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/settheory-early/
- 作者：José Ferreirós（*Labyrinth of Thought* 作者，主笔条目）。

### 3.1 动机排序：良序论战第一，悖论不是独立动机（txt 行 928–934）

> "**The impressive polemics which surrounded his Well-Ordering Theorem, and the most interesting and difficult problem posed by the foundations of mathematics, led Zermelo to concentrate on axiomatic set theory.** As a result of his incisive analysis, in 1908 he published his axiom system, **showing how it blocked the known paradoxes** and yet allowed for a masterful development of the theory of cardinals and ordinals."

注意：动机从句里**没有**把悖论列为独立动机；1908 对悖论的作用被 Ferreirós 表述为 **blocked**（挡住）。

### 3.2 Zermelo 对悖论的立场：dissolved（消解）（行 869–874）

> "Other authors, like Zermelo, believed that **most of those paradoxes dissolved as soon as one worked within a restricted axiomatic system.** They concentrated on the 'set-theoretic' paradoxes … and were led to search for axiomatic systems of set theory."

### 3.3 iterative conception 是 post facto（行 843–853）

> "the now famous iterative conception … was conspicuously absent from many of the relevant authors. … The iterative conception was first suggested by Kurt Gödel in [1933] … It came only post facto, after very substantial amounts of set theory had been developed and fully systematized."

**证据价值**：1908 年的公理化**没有**迭代概念可作哲学辩护——「ZFC 通过迭代概念解决罗素悖论」的叙事依赖的是 1933 之后的哲学解释（对照证据 03 的正则公理时间线：Mirimanoff 1917 / von Neumann 1925，同样晚于 1908）。

## 4. Hallett ——SEP「Zermelo 的集合论公理化」条目（全文在手，最核心）

- 本地副本：`搜索原始响应-20261017/sep-zermelo-set-theory-hallett.html/.txt`，sha256[:16]（html）= `1d3642ba7586fda7`。
- 来源 URL：https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/zermelo-set-theory/
- 作者：Michael Hallett（1984 专著 *Cantorian Set Theory and Limitation of Size* 作者；条目 2013 首版）。citation_author 元数据 = "Hallett, Michael"。

### 4.1 1908 论文真正证明的定理（行 446–460）

> "Zermelo shows that, on the basis of his system, the two central paradoxes, that of the greatest set and that of Russell, cannot arise. In fact, Zermelo proves: **Every set M possesses at least one subset M0 that is not an element of M.** (1908b: 265)"

> "The proof is an easy modification of the argument for Russell's Paradox, **using the contradiction this time as a reductio.** … **one person's contradiction is another person's reductio.**"

（对照：证据 01 CN-082；教科书对应 = 证据 02 §1.3 Enderton Theorem 2A；数学行对应 = C-433 `R_A ∉ A`。）

### 4.2 Zermelo 1908b: 265 ——「disposes of … so far as we are concerned」（行 566–573）

> "It follows from the theorem that not all objects x of the domain 𝔅 can be elements of one and the same set; that is, the domain 𝔅 is not itself a set, and **this disposes of the 'Russell antinomy' so far as we are concerned.** (1908b: 265)"

### 4.3 Zermelo 1908b: 262 ——未证一致性 + 悖论消失的自陈（行 584–597，**本文件最重引文**）

> "**I have not yet even been able to prove rigorously that my axioms are 'consistent', though this is certainly very essential; instead I have had to confine myself to pointing out now and then that the 'antinomies' discovered so far vanish one and all if the principles here proposed are adopted as a basis.** But I hope to have done at least some useful spadework hereby for subsequent investigations in such deeper problems. (1908b: 262)"

### 4.4 Hallett 的评注：未处理一般一致性（行 584–586、598–601）

> "**But although this deals with the Russell paradox and the paradox of the universal set, it does not tackle the general consistency of the system. Zermelo was well aware of this**, as is clear from the Introduction to his paper."

> "It should be remarked in passing that **Zermelo doesn't deal specifically with the Burali-Forti paradox either**, for the simple reason that it cannot be properly formulated in his system."

### 4.5 Zermelo 1908a: 128 ——exclude 措辞（行 1752–1757）

> "…this antinomy '**is without significance** for my point of view, since the principles I employed **exclude** the existence of a set W [of all ordinals]' (Zermelo 1908a: 128 …)"

## 5. 一手交叉验证：Hallett 英译 ↔ 我们的 Zermelo 1908b 德文 OCR

一手 OCR：`搜索原始响应-20261017/mineru_md/zermelo1908-pp261-281.md` 行 22（对应 1908b p. 262，Introduction）：

| 德文原文（一手 OCR，逐字） | Hallett 英译（SEP，逐字） |
|---|---|
| „Selbst die gewiß sehr wesentliche „**Widerspruchslosigkeit**" meiner Axiome habe ich **noch nicht streng beweisen können**" | "I have **not yet even been able to prove rigorously** that my axioms are '**consistent**'" |
| „sondern mich auf den gelegentlichen Hinweis beschränken müssen, daß die bisher bekannten „Antinomieen" **sämtlich verschwinden**, wenn man die hier vorgeschlagenen Prinzipien zugrunde legt" | "instead I have had to confine myself to pointing out now and then that the 'antinomies' discovered so far **vanish one and all** if the principles here proposed are adopted as a basis" |
| „Für spätere Untersuchungen … möchte ich hiermit wenigstens eine **nützliche Vorarbeit** liefern" | "I hope to have done at least some **useful spadework** hereby for subsequent investigations" |

**三方措辞族互证**：一手层 `verschwinden`（证据 01 CN-082 检索命中同一词族：ausschließen / verschwinden / Wegfall / beseitigt）；Ferreirós 用 dissolved/blocked；Hallett 用 vanish/disposes of/exclude。**没有任何一个权威层使用 solve/lösen 描述 1908 对悖论的作用**——与 CN-082 的原文穷尽检索结论（lösen 仅两处且不以悖论为主词）完全一致。

## 6. 对工作假说的意义（我们的解读，非文献事实）

工作假说：「ZFC 解决了罗素悖论」的说法当初**不存在**形式化证明；1908 论文的实际作为 = 公理删除/准入层拒绝 + 用罗素论证改造的 reductio 定理 + 「已知悖论消失」的观察性声明 + 明示未证一致性。

本文件新增的二手层证据链（三方独立）：

1. **Moore 1978**（摘要级）：usual interpretation 是「悖论动机」，Moore 论证主要动机是 1904 良序争议——「解决悖论」叙事在动机端就已被权威史学动摇；
2. **Ferreirós**（全文）：良序论战列为第一动机；悖论作用 = blocked/dissolved；迭代概念 post facto（1933）——「解决」的哲学辩护晚出 25 年；
3. **Hallett**（全文）：逐条给出 1908 论文实际证明的定理（`M0 ⊄ M`），Zermelo 自陈未证一致性、悖论 vanish、Burali-Forti 未处理，Hallett 明确写「it does not tackle the general consistency」。

一手德文 OCR ↔ Hallett 英译逐句吻合（§5），消除了「英译添油加醋」的疑虑。
