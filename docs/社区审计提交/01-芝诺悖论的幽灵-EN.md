---

## 0. Conclusions first

**In one sentence**: so that "isomorphic things can be treated as the same" (univalence), HoTT changed "sameness" from a yes-or-no **fact** into **data** about which one can keep asking, level after level, "in which ways are these the same?". We designed a task that depends specifically on this trade-off and found that, in a world where sameness is a fact, it is written down in one line, while in HoTT every level of rules you add makes the next level appear. This has the same shape as Zeno's paradox.

In six points:

1. **What is to be written down**: semi-simplicial structure, that is, "gluing shapes level by level out of points, segments, triangles and tetrahedra, with the boundaries of boundaries matching up". In classical mathematics this is a one-line definition.
2. **The world where sameness is a fact** (Lean 4, where an equality has only one proof): this one line is the complete definition, and all the "matching up" requirements hold automatically [Mathematical fact].
3. **In HoTT** (Cubical Agda): the same one-line definition passes the checker as well, but it accepts data that "do not match up". There is an example in which every boundary equation holds, yet two simplification routes that ought to agree wind around the circle once and twice, respectively [Mathematical fact].
4. **Adding the "matching up" requirement**: there is more than one way to add it; once it is added, the "matching up" at the next level can fail again [Mathematical fact, instances].
5. **How many levels must be added is determined by how many levels "sameness" has**: one level suffices when every level is a set, two levels suffice when every level is a groupoid, and in the universe the number of levels of "sameness" has no upper bound [Mathematical fact, instances].
6. **Every concrete level can be written down** (we machine-checked up to level 5, generated level by level by an external program), but nobody has written down inside HoTT a uniform definition "for all levels at once"; this has been an open problem for more than ten years [Source report]; all known successful approaches add back into the theory a "sameness as a fact" [Source report].

**Why this is a Zeno-like candidate**: with Zeno, every step can be taken, every step leaves a distance still to go, and the walk is never finished; the difficulty disappears only if one goes back on the premise that the theory changed (that space and time are infinitely divisible). Here, every level can be added, every level leaves a next level, and a uniform definition cannot be written inside the theory; the difficulty disappears only if one goes back to "sameness is a fact". This is an analogy of structure, not the referent of the user's 2026-09-27 phrase "the ghost of Zeno's paradox."

**The user's overall verdict** [Original words, 2026-09-27]: "本repo复活了芝诺悖论的幽灵和罗素悖论的幽灵，并且找到的HoTT理论的问题。" (Translation: "This repo has revived the ghost of Zeno's paradox and the ghost of Russell's paradox, and has found the problem of the HoTT theory.") On 2026-10-01 the user clarified that the Zeno phrase refers to the circle paradox and the repository's subsequent discussion and analysis of it. A7 infinite coherence is a separate Zeno-like candidate proposed by an AI, not the referent of that verdict. This paper presents A7's own formal results, interpretation and open questions for community review; the overall verdict does not answer this paper's two open questions one by one.

**The most important reservation**: **impossibility has not been proved**. "A uniform definition cannot be written in book HoTT" is an open problem. As soon as someone gives a uniform definition inside book HoTT, the strong form of this paper is withdrawn at once.

**English summary.** Univalence turns "isomorphic structures are identical" into a rule, at a price: sameness stops being a yes/no fact and becomes data, so one can keep asking "in which way are these the same?". We test this trade-off with a task built to depend on it: define semi-simplicial structure (points, face maps, "faces of faces agree"). In a world where sameness is a mere fact (Lean 4, UIP) the one-line definition is complete and its coherence holds by `rfl`. In HoTT (Cubical Agda) the same line accepts incoherent data (two routes on the circle wind once and twice). Adding the hexagon coherence as data gives non-unique fillers, and the next coherence can fail. The number of levels needed grows with the truncation level: one for sets, two for groupoids, unbounded in the universe. Every finite level can be written (machine-checked up to level 5), but an internal definition uniform in n is a well-known open problem, and every known solution adds back a strict, "factual" equality. This mirrors Zeno: every step is doable, each leaves a next step, the whole is not completed inside the theory. **Impossibility is not proved; it is open.** No inconsistency of HoTT is claimed.

## 1. Where this line comes from: an idea about what a paradox is

The initiator's idea is the following (verbatim, core-cognition ledger KC-000047):

[Initiator's words]
> 理论抽象，为了理论的经济性（比如朴素集合论不考虑时间因素导致罗素悖论）和工具性（为了普适性而必须理想化），必然会引入非现实的理论元素。比如说，现实是运动-时空不连续的（量子化的），而数轴、实数是具备稠密性的，也就是无限可分的，也正是因为稠密性，所以引入了芝诺悖论（每次走一半，永远走不完）、圆环悖论（M和N）。这些非现实元素，其实在数理逻辑上可以被看成是合取推断中的前提，这些前提因为是非现实的，所以会在理论推演中导致非现实的推演结果，与现实实际现象相矛盾——也就是理论中的悖论现象。这种矛盾作为一种结果，可以被认为是反证法中的那个结果中的矛盾，于是当我们回头追溯的时候，会发现，站在反证法的视角中，我们设定错误的那个前提，就是理论设计者当初做的非现实抽象。

Translation:

> Theoretical abstraction, for the sake of the theory's economy (for example, naive set theory does not take time into account, which leads to Russell's paradox) and its instrumentality (it must idealize for the sake of generality), inevitably introduces non-real theoretical elements. For example, in reality motion and space-time are discontinuous (quantized), whereas the number line and the real numbers are dense, that is, infinitely divisible; and precisely because of density, Zeno's paradox (each time you walk half of the way, you never finish walking) and the ring paradox (M and N) are introduced. In mathematical logic these non-real elements can in fact be regarded as premises in a conjunctive inference; because these premises are non-real, they lead, in the theory's derivations, to non-real results that contradict the actual phenomena of reality — that is, to the phenomenon of paradox in the theory. This contradiction, as a result, can be regarded as the contradiction in the result of a proof by contradiction; so when we trace back, we find that, from the standpoint of proof by contradiction, the premise we set wrongly is the non-real abstraction that the theory's designers made in the first place.

Next comes the initiator's requirement on "how to search" (verbatim, KC-000048):

[Initiator's words]
> 悖论对理论的攻击角度，比如芝诺悖论对理论的攻击角度，是紧紧瞄准了稠密性来的，也就是时空稠密的假设这个（可能的）理论的非现实元素。罗素悖论对理论的攻击角度，是瞄准了构造是一个过程这件事来的，而朴素集合论忽略了过程，只关注结果。所以对理论的非现实性元素导致的问题（也就是悖论）的查找，要使用针对性的策略思路，而不是蛮干。

Translation:

> The angle from which a paradox attacks a theory — for example, the angle from which Zeno's paradox attacks the theory — is aimed squarely at density, that is, at the assumption that space-time is dense, a (possibly) non-real element of the theory. The angle from which Russell's paradox attacks the theory is aimed at the fact that construction is a process, whereas naive set theory ignores the process and attends only to the result. So the search for the problems caused by a theory's non-real elements (that is, for paradoxes) must use a targeted strategy, not brute force.

In plain words:

- To be convenient (to save effort, to unify, to be general), a theory always changes some condition of reality.
- The changed condition becomes a "premise" in the reasoning. Since this premise does not match reality, the results derived from it may contradict reality: that is a paradox.
- Read the other way round: when a paradox appears, the abstraction that "changed a condition of reality" in the first place is at fault.
- So one should not search for paradoxes at random, but **first see what the theory changed in order to be convenient, then design a task that "needs that condition", so that the change shows**. Zeno aimed at "infinitely divisible", Russell at "construction is a process".

Two further statements fix the place of this line (KC-000010, KC-000022): what the initiator is looking for is not a contradiction inside the theory but "the theory's non-reality" (original words: "重点不是要在理论本身中找到矛盾，而是找到理论的非现实性"; translation: "the point is not to find a contradiction in the theory itself, but to find the theory's non-reality"); and the initiator distinguishes two situations: in the first, **it can be completed in reality, yet cannot be completed in the theory**; in the second, it cannot be completed in reality (it does not halt), yet the theory bypasses the prior question "can it be completed?" and pretends that it has been completed. This paper belongs to the first kind; the sister paper "The ghost of Russell's paradox" belongs to the second.

**On "reality"** [Judgment]: "reality" here includes actual mathematical practice and programs that actually run, and also what the initiator calls "imagined reality" (KC-000044). "Non-real" only means "the theory changed a condition of reality on which completing something depends"; it does not mean that the theory is wrong or inconsistent. This viewpoint is not a standard position in the philosophy of mathematics; this paper states it as it is, as **a research framework awaiting audit**, without presupposing that it holds.

## 2. The trade-off we target: univalence turns "sameness" from a fact into a structure

HoTT has a basic rule called **univalence**: isomorphic things can be treated as the same. Mathematicians do this all the time anyway (it is called "abuse of notation"); HoTT makes it a formal rule. The benefit is real: many facts of the kind "the structure is invariant under isomorphism" no longer have to be proved one by one.

The price: **in HoTT, "two things are the same" is no longer a yes-or-no question but a piece of data about "in which ways they are the same"**.

A minimal example: the Boolean type Bool has two isomorphisms to itself, the identity and the swap of true and false. In HoTT, "Bool is the same as itself" therefore has two different proofs. They really are different: transporting `true` along the second proof gives `false`; transporting along the first still gives `true` [Mathematical fact, claim C-63, a standard result, HoTT Book Example 3.1.9].

Further up: between these two proofs one can ask again "in which ways are they the same", and further up still one can keep asking. Level after level, there is no natural end.

In HoTT, a "set" means precisely that **this questioning stops at the first level** (any two proofs that two elements are the same coincide, so there is at most one). Even the universe that contains all types is not a set, and neither is "the universe that contains all sets" [Mathematical fact, C-63, C-71].

In reality, "sameness" is mostly just a fact: check once, yes or no. This is the condition of reality that the theory changed, and it is the target of this line.

## 3. The task we designed: writing down "semi-simplicial structure"

**What it is**: semi-simplicial structure is data for "assembling shapes from triangles". Dimension zero is points, dimension one segments, dimension two triangles, dimension three tetrahedra, and so on upwards. Each block has its "faces" (its boundary), and **the faces of faces must match up** (whichever face you take first and whichever next, the results agree as prescribed).

**In classical mathematics**: this is a one-line definition. A family of sets X₀, X₁, X₂, …, together with face maps d, and the face identities `d_i d_j = d_{j-1} d_i` (i < j).

**Why we chose it**: its definition is full of requirements that "two simplification routes must agree", and "agree" is exactly "be the same". Once "sameness" turns from a fact into data, these requirements themselves demand additional data, so this task touches precisely the trade-off we are targeting.

**What the "hexagon" is**: taking three faces in succession, there are two different routes that reduce the three face maps to the same result. Each route consists of three steps of "applying a face identity", and together the two routes form something like a hexagon. Requiring the two routes to agree is "hexagon coherence".

---

## 4. The same task, two worlds, different results

### 4.1 In the world where "sameness is a fact": one line is enough

In Lean 4, an equality has only one proof ("proof irrelevance"); the two routes are two proofs of the same equation and are therefore automatically equal. Theorem `coh2`: for every semi-simplicial structure the hexagon holds automatically; the proof is just `rfl`, and it depends on no axiom [Mathematical fact, claim C-65; this is a statement in the world where "sameness is a fact", not a statement of HoTT].

That is: in this world, the one-line definition is the complete definition, and all higher requirements that "faces of faces match up" hold automatically.

### 4.2 In HoTT: the same one-line definition accepts data that "do not match up"

In Cubical Agda (an implementation of HoTT), the same one-line definition passes the checker as well. But we constructed an example: take the circle at every level, the identity as face maps, and "going once around" as the single boundary equation. Every face identity holds, yet of the two routes of the hexagon, one winds once around the circle and the other twice (both numbers are computed directly). So hexagon coherence **fails** [Mathematical fact, claim C-64].

So in HoTT, the line "points plus face maps plus face identities" is not the complete definition.

### 4.3 Adding "matching up": not unique, and the next level appears

So "the two routes agree" has to be added as new data (hexagon coherence). The problem is:

- There is more than one way to add it. On a special structure there are two different ways: a trivial one, and one that wraps once around the sphere [Mathematical fact, claim C-66].
- Once it is added, the consistency **at the next level** (involving P₄, the permutohedron on four letters, a polytope with 14 faces, generated in the machine by an external program from the rewriting graph on four letters) holds for one of the ways and fails for the other [Mathematical fact, claims C-66, C-68].

Each time a level is added, the requirement of the next level grows out of it.

### 4.4 How many levels must be added is determined by how many levels "sameness" has

- When every level is a set: the hexagon holds automatically; adding the first level is enough [Mathematical fact, claims C-64, C-70].
- When every level is a groupoid ("sameness" has at most two levels): the hexagon itself becomes a yes-or-no proposition, and the consistency of the second level holds automatically [Mathematical fact, claim C-70].
- In the universe the number of levels of "sameness" has no upper bound, so there is no end.

The general rule "when every level has t levels, coherence must be added up to level t+1" is a meta-level argument made from the instances above [Judgment]; this paper has not mechanized it.

### 4.5 Every level can be written down, but not "all levels at once"

Every concrete level can be written down by an external program, level by level, together with a trivial inhabitant. We machine-checked levels 0 to 5 [Mathematical fact, claim C-62]. That it can be written for any fixed n comes from the literature [Source report]; it is not a proof in this repository.

But **writing, inside HoTT, a definition uniform in the variable n**, i.e., a type family `F : ℕ → U₁` such that F(n) encodes the n-truncated semi-simplicial types, is a recognized open problem [Source report, see section 6]: the literature has neither a uniform definition in book HoTT nor a proof of impossibility.

### 4.6 It bites the theory itself: the interpreter does not fit into the universe

HoTT would like to interpret itself, for example by interpreting "syntax truncated to a set" into the universe, and then the target must be a set; but the universe is not a set, and neither is the universe of sets [Mathematical fact, C-63, C-71]. In the world where "sameness is a fact", this route is open [Mathematical fact, C-72, Lean 4].

A toy syntax shows this in miniature: without truncation there is a faithful interpretation, but the syntax itself is not a set; truncated to a set, it has no faithful interpretation [Mathematical fact, claim C-67]. **This is a miniature, not a real syntax**: its "swap" equation is meant from the start to be a non-trivial identification, whereas all the equations of a real type-theory syntax can be interpreted by `refl`; so the fairer obstacle is the one in the previous paragraph, "the target must be a set" (the `REVISIONS.md` in that package's directory says so).

Kraus (LICS 2021) writes: "One possible view is that, unlike many other programming languages, HoTT cannot serve as its own interpreter with this approach" [Source report].

## 5. Correspondence with Zeno

| | Zeno (the initiator's reading) | This line |
|---|---|---|
| Why the theory is convenient | Motion is put on the continuous number line and computed with limits | "Sameness" is treated as a structure, so invariance comes for free (univalence) |
| The condition of reality that was changed | Motion and space-time are discrete → the number line is infinitely divisible | "Sameness" is a settled fact → "sameness" has infinitely many levels |
| The process aimed at it | Each time, walk half of what remains | Each time one goes up a dimension, coherence must be added for the "sameness" of the level below |
| Each finite step | Each halving can be done | Each concrete level can be written down |
| What each step leaves | A remaining distance | A remaining level of coherence |
| The whole | In the step-by-step description, the walk is never finished | A uniform definition inside the theory still cannot be written (open problem) |
| The theory's own remedy | Limits (an infinity treated as already walked) | No known remedy inside book HoTT |
| After going back on the premise | Discrete model: finished in finitely many steps | "Sameness" is a fact (UIP, the set level, the outer layer of two-level type theory): done in one line |

**What is borrowed and what is not** [Judgment]: only one group of relations is borrowed: each finite step is feasible, each step leaves a remainder, the whole is not completed, and the difficulty disappears once one goes back on the premise. This correspondence does not claim that this line and Zeno's paradox are formally equivalent.

## 6. State of the literature: the open problem in the original words

The original sentences below were checked one by one against a full-text library by the original researcher on 2026-09-26; this paper reproduces them as they are, **and the originals were not checked again while this paper was being written**; auditors are asked to recheck them.

"…even this problem is still unsolved: every attempt to internally encode the combinatorics that generate the type of A_n, as a function of n, seems to lead once again to an infinite regress."
- **Kraus, *Internal ∞-Categorical Models of Dependent Type Theory* (LICS 2021)**:
  - Abstract: "In homotopy type theory (HoTT) however, it is a long-standing and frequently discussed open problem whether the type theory 'eats itself' and can serve as its own interpreter.   - Example 6: "In HoTT, the [lowest or higher] universe U is not a set. … One possible view is that, unlike many other programming languages, HoTT cannot serve as its own interpreter with this approach."

- **Buchholtz, *Higher Structures in Homotopy Type Theory* (Springer 2019)**, p. 162: "…no-one has figured out how to do it, and at an informal poll of HoTT-researchers in Warsaw in 2015 a majority believed that it is impossible." - **Altenkirch, Capriotti, Kraus, *Extending HoTT with Strict Equality* (CSL 2016)**: "Defining n-truncated semi-simplicial types as a family SST : ℕ → U in homotopy type theory is a famous open problem. … a clear majority of the participants expected it to be impossible." (The same informal poll as the one recorded by Buchholtz.)
- **Annenkov, Capriotti, Kraus, Sattler, *Two-Level Type Theory and Applications* (MSCS 2023)**: the abstract says that for any externally fixed natural number n, semi-simplicial types up to level n can be constructed in HoTT, "Such results cannot be expressed in HoTT itself…"; to obtain internally a "universe of semi-simplicial types", an axiom making the inner and outer natural numbers isomorphic is needed ((A1), together with (A2), (A3)).
- **Part, Luo 2015**: "Voevodsky proposed a definition of SSTs in Homotopy Type System (HTS), an extension of HoTT with non-fibrant types, including an extensional strict equality type."
- **Herbelin, Ramachandra (MSCS 2025)**: uniform definitions at the set level (augmented semi-simplicial sets and semi-cubical sets), formalized in Coq.
- **The most recent threat: Herbelin, Ramachandra, arXiv:2602.12689 (2026-02) §6**: "On HSet, coh²_FRAME is trivially provable and the construction stops here. If we were working in an untruncated universe U, the construction would continue recursively… Such an infinite process can again be represented using streams… This would solve the semi-simplicial type problem." This is an outlook, not a completed construction.
- **Summary of the current state** [Source report]: the search found neither a uniform definition in book HoTT nor a proof of impossibility. The known successful definitions are all in extended theories: two-level type theory (2LTT) and Voevodsky's HTS, logic-enriched HoTT, and displayed type theory (dTT).

## 7. Attribution: which premise is at fault

**This section is one of the main topics of this paper.** The initiator explicitly requires that, once the phenomenon is there, "which premise exactly" be discussed carefully with the initiator, and not put off with "we'll come back to it later" (KC-000049, 2026-09-24). So four things are written out here: which candidate premises there are; the competing attribution for each; what evidence can tell them apart; our judgment and its status.

**Candidate premises** (each is either "a trade-off the theory made in order to be convenient" or "a remedy it did not make"):

- **A. Univalence**: "sameness" in the universe is equivalence, so the universe is not a set.
- **B. Intensional equality**: the language has no "strict equality" (as in Martin-Löf type theory without the K rule).
- **C. Every type is "fibrant"**: there is no factual layer. What two-level type theory and HTS add is precisely the non-fibrant layer.
- **D. The inner natural numbers are not tied to the outer (factual) natural numbers.**
- **E. The uniformity requirement**: the definition must be written inside the theory, uniformly in the variable n.
- **Reality-side premise**: in reality, "faces of faces match up" is a fact settled once and for all.

**Competing attributions and the evidence that can tell them apart**:

1. **"The cause is B, which ordinary type theory has too"**: Martin-Löf type theory without K can consistently be extended with K, after which every type is a set and the regress stops. HoTT cannot do this, because univalence implies that the universe is not a set, and not even the universe of sets is (C-63, C-71). So what is special about HoTT is that A **closes** that way out. [Mathematical fact plus judgment]
2. **A control by truncation level**: the length of the regress is determined by the number of levels of "sameness" at each level: at sets it stops at the first step (C-64), at groupoids at the second step (C-70), and on the sphere the second level can fail to be fillable (C-66, C-68). This locates the cause at "the number of levels of sameness", rather than at "intensional equality" in general. [Mathematical fact, instances]
3. **What the repairs must add back**: two-level type theory needs an outer strict equality (removing C), plus an axiom tying the outer natural numbers to the inner ones (removing D); with strict equality alone, without tying the natural numbers, one can only write the n-th level for each outer n. [Source report]
4. **"The cause is E: the uniformity requirement is too strong"**: a uniform definition is exactly what "defining a concept" means. In Zeno, too, every halving can be done; our section 4.5 corresponds precisely to that half. [Judgment]
5. **"No premise is wrong; a good idea is simply still missing"**: a uniform definition appearing in book HoTT would overturn this paper's candidate, and a proof of impossibility would confirm it; at present there is neither. [Source report plus judgment]

**Our judgment** [Judgment, conditional]:

- What is at work is "inside the theory there is only structural sameness, and on the universe the number of its levels has no upper bound", i.e., A and C together.
- The known repairs must also tie the natural numbers (D). This shows that what the theory still lacks inside is a factual, level-by-level counting carried out outside the theory, like our external program that prints the levels one by one.
- Correspondence with the initiator's theory: univalence is exactly the abstraction HoTT made for the sake of economy (the "economy" slot of KC-000047); the infinite regress is its non-real consequence; every repair adds back factual sameness, which is exactly the backward trace of "站在反证法的视角，我们设定错误的那个前提，就是理论设计者当初做的非现实抽象" ("from the standpoint of proof by contradiction, the premise we set wrongly is the non-real abstraction that the theory's designers made in the first place"). This is a **correspondence**, not a proof of the kind "predicted first, then hit".
- Condition: the open problem stays open, or receives a negative answer.

**Status of the attribution**: all of the above is [Judgment]; none of it is a machine proof. The initiator has the final say in the discussion of the attribution.

## 8. The strongest objections, and conditions for withdrawal

1. **"Nobody has thought of it yet"**: this is the biggest risk of this paper, and an explicit condition for withdrawal. As soon as a uniform definition of semi-simplicial types appears in book HoTT (including a realization in book HoTT of the outlook in §6 of Herbelin–Ramachandra 2026), the strong form is withdrawn.
2. **"Physically, structural sameness is closer to reality"** [Source report]: in higher gauge symmetry, weak 2-groups are considered closer to physics (Liu–Luo–Wang, SciPost Phys. 2025); in practice people often switch to strict 2-groups (crossed modules) for convenience (Bullivant et al., Rev. Math. Phys. 2019). Strictification is an equivalence for 2-groups; for 3-groups only semi-strict versions or special cases have been achieved. So "in reality, sameness eventually settles into a fact" is not self-evident in this field. [Judgment] Even there, the method used in practice to cut off the regress is precisely to add back factual sameness at the level of the laws, which points in the same direction as this paper's attribution; but this does not answer the question on the initiator's behalf.
3. **"This is just an instance of general computability limits"** [Judgment]: no. It depends on proof-relevant equality, and it disappears under UIP (C-65, C-72).
4. **"Just use sets instead"** [Judgment]: that changes the task. What classical mathematics accomplishes with strict laws is semi-simplicial spaces whose values are homotopy types, not semi-simplicial sets. Semi-simplicial sets pose no such difficulty in HoTT: when every level is a set, the first-level hexagon coherence holds automatically (`setsCohere` of C-64); that all higher levels hold automatically is an extrapolation of the same argument ("path spaces are contractible"), and this repository has machine-proved only the first level (C-70 also proves the second level for groupoid levels).
5. **"'Zeno-like' is only an analogy"** [Judgment]: this paper claims only that one group of relations holds: each finite step is feasible, each step leaves a next step, the whole is not completed inside the theory, and the difficulty disappears once one goes back on the premise. Whether this group of relations suffices to be called Zeno-like "cannot be completed" is a judgment in the philosophy of mathematics; see section 10.

## Appendix D: Recheck of the Lean negative controls (self-check of 2026-09-27, recorded in this version)

has type   LeF (weaken i) (weaken j)

That is, the error falls on the **argument** of this step (the order proof points the wrong way); it is reported by the elaborator, and the term never reaches the kernel.
