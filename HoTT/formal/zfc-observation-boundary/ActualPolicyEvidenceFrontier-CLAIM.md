`ActualPolicyWitness`, and cannot license a formal reductio.  The proof is a
status-ledger classification only; it does not prove that new evidence cannot
fill any field.

## Reopen conditions

The ledger changes only if a source supplies one of the missing fields:

1. a specified community's direct adoption of the UOU-like P as its relevant
   ZFC-plus-P policy;
2. a ZFC-level completion-observation policy and its actual absence;
3. a source-defined same-P route to the specified HoTT B; or
4. formal definitions of A and B together with a proof or adopted axiom of
   their incompatibility.

None of these conditions has yet been supplied by the frozen source pack.
