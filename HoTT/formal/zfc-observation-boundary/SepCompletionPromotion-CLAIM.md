Source: [SepCompletionPromotion.lean](SepCompletionPromotion.lean).

## Exact scope

This file does **not** prove that SEP's account is mathematically or physically
correct, that standard topology really completes a supertask, or that ZFC
adopts any policy. It machine-checks a finite transcription/classification of
the frozen SEP source card used in H103.

The card records:

```text
F: geometric convergence to 1 in standard real topology      = stated
D: Achilles completes every supertask step                   = stated
promotion F → D                                               = stated
verified task-preserving bridge on the frozen card            = not supplied
final-action completion                                        = excluded
```

The three no-axiom Lean theorems prove that this card meets the project's
definition of an `ACTUAL_P_CANDIDATE_SOURCE_MAPPED` classification and that it
does not establish final-action completion.

The external source remains [SEP, “Supertasks,” §1.1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/spacetime-supertasks/), especially the statements around its Zeno walk, convergence, every-step completion and final-action distinction. The source card was frozen in [H103](../../../audit/20261004-P-DAG-ZFC-QP-103-PROMPT.md).
