## 1. reasoning #1（`:791`）—— 我首先做了什么判断

原文要点（逐字）：

> "The user is now asking a direct, substantive question: what do I think about '机器统观' …
> And they point me to the essay file … This is a judgment/opinion question grounded in the project."

我做的三个自我约束（原文）：

1. **不凭已有印象回答**：虽然"my assessment, which I've largely formed through the audit"，但
   用户**明确指向**了那篇文章，所以"I should read it to ground my answer"。
2. **区分阐释层与原文权威**："the essay is the AI interpretation layer, essay-role:v1，
   核心认知.md is the only user-original authority."
3. **避免把方法论判断说成数学结论**："The MATH_PROOF_BEFORE_DELIVERY gate applies to delivering
   math conclusions — my answer is an assessment of methodology, not a math claim, so I must be
   careful to phrase it as assessment, distinguishing observed/confirmed/candidate."

**为什么这么想**：这条提问不是"查事实"，是"要我的判断"。对判断题，我第一动作是确认判断的
**证据基础**是否已加载——而不是直接输出观点。这也是项目 AGENTS 的硬要求（essay 是 AI 阐释层，
不能当用户原文权威）。

## 3. reasoning #4（`:824`）—— 核心判断的形成（全文 6447 字符，这里是关键链条）

这条推理是本回合的全部思维。我按它的内部顺序还原：

### 3.1 先给机器统观下准确定性（锚定我的审计 A3）

> "What 机器统观 actually is (per my audit A3): a declared-grammar calibrator — exhaustive
> enumeration over finite hand-declared grammars + grammar-preserving reduction + native kernel
> verification + receipts. Its safety property (enumerable denominator, remainder=0) is real."

**为什么这么想**：我对"是否最合理"的第一步不是比较选项，而是**先把对象是什么钉死**。
这是审计期间我独立复算过的（selftest 94/94、search.py 契约、L1-v2 界、registry M2="no general
dependent/higher-path search"）。定性为"校准器"是后续一切判断的前提。

### 3.2 把对象与目标对撞，得出结构性结论

> "The core tension: The goal (finding a NEW paradox witness in HoTT) requires candidate
> generation. The engine verifies but doesn't generate. … A finite declared grammar can only
> produce candidates within the declared denominator — '分类法即命运' (GLM B3)."

**为什么这么想**：这里我做了一个**对撞**——把"引擎的安全性质"（分母可枚举、不越界）和
"研究目标的需要"（需要**新**候选）放在一起。对撞的结果是：安全性质同时就是能力边界。
这个推论是**结构性**的（分母由人工声明），所以我在正文里明确写"这不是实现程度问题，是结构问题"。
GLM 的 B3"分类法即命运"被我复核成立并引用为同构证据。

### 3.3 检验反方：机器统观为什么仍然必要

推理里我列了四条**支持**机器统观的理由（原文）：

> - "Without machine proofs, any 'paradox found' is just a story (KC-000021 demands Lean/actual runs)."
> - "The programmatic-completeness discipline (omission audits, remainder=0, holdout) is exactly what
>   prevents the R1 failure mode (infinite accumulation of bounded negatives that sound like progress)."
> - "HoTT's own difficulty: it's designed to be paradox-resistant, so casual semantic candidates will
>   almost surely be type-errors or already-known boundaries. Machine verification is the only way to
>   distinguish 'new paradox' from 'my confusion.'"
> - "The eight-axis tensor + generator families is genuinely the right *shape* of a systematic search."

**为什么这么想**：我刻意没有只做单向批评。在做"它不能发现"这个判断的同时，我**主动构造了
支持它的最强论据**（这是我在正文里写成"第一部分：它是最合理的，甚至是唯一合理的"的原因）。
正因为这一侧成立，"发现侧缺失"才是**遗憾**而不是**错误**——这个区分决定了我整篇的语气。

### 3.4 定位用户自身设定里的张力

> "…there's a tension within the user's own instructions: use LLM pattern-matching (KC-000007) but
> don't be captured by prior cognition (KC-000017)."

**为什么这么想**：我读五片时发现文章自身把两种对 LLM 的期待并列（KC-000007 期待模式匹配解题、
KC-000017 警告训练数据先验有害）。我没有把它当成文章的自相矛盾来批评，而是理解为
"一个真实的、需要被工程方法解开的张力"，并给出我的解法：

> "机器统观其实是对这个张力的一个工程回答：让语义供给（人或 AI 的启发式）负责提出候选，
> 让机器负责证伪和收据。这个分工是对的。问题只在于：当前构建只造了后一半。"

### 3.5 正面提案，并标注为判断

> "my synthesis: 机器统观 is the most reasonable path **as the spine** … but it is incomplete as a
> discovery method … The most reasonable *complete* path is a two-engine structure:
> - Semantic/engine-driven discovery: the essay's redirects (ASK, direction B, economics,
>   self-reference, expressivity) as *search heuristics over the eight axes* …
> - Machine verification: what exists now.
> The bottleneck is the former, and the current plan spends M0–M6 mostly on the latter
> (PSJ/TGSQ/R4X are all verification/promotion lines, not generation lines). That's the substance
> of my A3."

**为什么这么想**：我要求自己**不只是诊断，还要给出正面形态**。正面提案的形状是"把五个语义重定向
当作八轴上的搜索策略"，而不是自由生成——因为自由生成会破坏引擎的安全设计。这个提案在正文中
被标为 `assessment`，不是已证结论。

### 3.6 诚实保留未知

> - "Whether a genuinely new HoTT paradox exists (open — the project correctly does not presuppose
>   HoTT is inconsistent)."
> - "Whether '最合理' is even decidable without knowing the alternative considered. I should compare
>   against plausible alternatives: (a) pure LLM 模式匹配 without machine proof — rejected by
>   KC-000021 …; (b) pure paper-based math philosophy — can't deliver machine proof;
>   (c) 机器统观 as built — best discipline but generation-poor."
> - "Risk: over-engineering. GLM B4 (infrastructure preference) — the project may keep building
>   verification infrastructure because it's tractable, while the discovery side stays unfunded.
>   This is my genuine concern, and it's the sharpest honest thing I can say."

**为什么这么想**："是否最合理"这个问句本身需要一个**比较集**才能诚实地回答。我明确意识到
"我没有穷尽所有可能路径"，所以在正文第五部分保留了"我无法断言不存在一个我没想到的更好路径"。

### 3.7 表达策略

> "I should be direct, structured, honest, including disagreement with parts of the project's
> implicit bet. … this is a '求全' user who values depth. I'll write a structured answer with
> clear evidence-status labels."

我预先定了正文结构（拆三问 → 三层判断 → 证据基础 → 与文章的关系 → 正面方向 → 我不声称的），
并且**明确写下"Let me be careful about math claims: I make no math claims."**
