2.2. Extension types along coﬁbrations. Finally, we come to the reason for
introducing this whole three-layer theory: extension types along c oﬁbrations. As
our notion of “coﬁbration” we use a shape inclusion, i.e. a pair of shapes {t :I |φ}
and {t : I |ψ } in the same cube such that t : I |φ ⊢ ψ . We will sometimes
abbreviate this as {t :I |φ} ⊆ { t :I |ψ }.
