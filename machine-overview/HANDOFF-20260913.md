The project had repeatedly explored time as stage/order/availability while the
user explicitly required a separate treatment of time, spacetime and motion
structure. The L3 TaskSpec therefore starts from an activity whose exact phase
changes from Pending to Done, then explicitly tests one theoryization:
represent that phase as an internal Cubical-dimension-indexed term.

This does not assume a dense real line, physical continuity or Planck-scale
discreteness. It asks which coherence is added by the selected Path structure.

## 17. Recommended next work

The next highest-value research increment has three connected fronts:

1. **Relative-completeness theorem:** freeze one small calculus and candidate
   class, define a candidate-to-grammar normal form, and prove that the
   reduction preserves typing, consumer, observation, completion behavior and
   reality-map data. This converts one bounded enumeration claim into a
   meaningful class-relative coverage theorem.
2. **R2 completion boundary:** replace `Program = ℕ → Instr` with a finite
   program syntax, define decoding and a universal bounded simulator, and prove
   the exact reduction needed for universal halting undecidability. One fixed
   divergent program is insufficient.
3. **Exact HoTT Gödel object level:** freeze a HoTT/2LTT object calculus and
   prove finite syntax, substitution, proof enumeration, arithmetic
   interpretation and strong representability. The author Coq replay supplies a
   precise comparison target: its `Q_incomplete` route cannot be imported by
   merely renaming its first-order theory as HoTT.

Both fronts must continue to ask whether a result depends on HoTT/Cubical
structure and whether the same task has a reality-relative correspondence.
Generic computability and incompleteness results do not satisfy those two
obligations by themselves.

In parallel with a later evaluation phase, full M4 needs an independently
sourced task or independent proposer. The current same-designer M4-lite remains
descriptive even though its v2 snapshot replay is evolution-safe.

Main-line integration remains separate from this research worktree. It
requires choosing exact paths to stage and deciding how the research extensions
enter STATE/projections and checkpoint receipts. The worktree capture
precondition itself is now fixed and regression-tested.
