## 2. Research question and screening contract

This sub-batch asks a source question, not a mathematical accusation: how does Zermelo's 1908 axiomatization state its set-formation permissions, especially Separation/Aussonderung and Power Set, and what does the paper itself make available for later operations?

The screen must distinguish:

1. what this historical source explicitly defines or postulates;
2. what a later first-order ZF/ZFC formulation changes or adds;
3. whether this source contains a fixed `u / formation / consumer / operation / observation / Done` relation that is relevant to Pattern P;
4. a historical or conceptual pressure point from an ordinary actual ZFC consumer.

The last three are not presumed. A reference to Russell, a restriction of comprehension, an existence axiom, or the word *Menge* cannot itself be promoted to ZFC Q.
