## HMZ-Z-014 — `Power` / hierarchy is a typed construction, not a free ZFC-side reentry

```text
source:
  Werner 1997 §4.4; archive Axioms.v:428–510, Hierarchy.v:77–136, Omega.v:175–178.
u:
  an existing Ens E and its Power E.
formation F:
  Power pattern-matches on sup A f and quantifies over P : A -> Prop;
  it constructs a set of bounded comprehension results.
consumer C:
  proof of Power axioms / small-set closure / hierarchy expressions.
P2/P3 check:
  no source describes E or Power E as awaiting its own legitimacy while being
  re-entered by the formation operator.  The host CIC typechecks P and the
  construction is explicitly a model-layer representation.
control:
  the power operation here relies on impredicative Prop and CIC's own formation
  rules; it cannot be silently relabelled as a bare ZFC Power Set phenomenon.
disposition:
  POWERSET_CONSTRUCTION_BRIDGE_CONTROL / ANTI_ANALOGY_CONTROL.
```
