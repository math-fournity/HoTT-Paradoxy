# HMZ-018：有界发现

The community antecedent map now extends beyond totality and vicious circles. Constructive logic/type theory explicitly treats existence as program-bearing delivery with termination and correctness evidence. The broad claim that foundational work never distinguishes formal existence from usable construction is therefore unsupported.

However, that does not make a classical ZFC statement an internal failure. Palmgren supplies a different foundational contract; ZF sources do not promise its program-like Done, and HoTT controls show that even type-theoretic systems can use noncomputational axioms. The missing question remains sharply source-dependent: does a real ZFC consumer itself promise a constructive-like delivery and then use an object before its source has paid that delivery?

```text
COMMUNITY_ANTECEDENT_TO_EXISTENCE/DELIVERY: YES
ZFC INTERNAL P5 VIOLATION: NOT FOUND
USER-P PREEMPTIVE CONSUMER CONDITION: NOT ESTABLISHED
P-QUALIFIED ZFC Q: NO
RUN DISPOSITION: P_REQUALIFICATION_REQUIRED
```

This run refines, rather than closes, the project: any future P claim must distinguish the known constructive delivery contract from the stronger claim of a same-theory, unpaid, preemptive consumer use.
