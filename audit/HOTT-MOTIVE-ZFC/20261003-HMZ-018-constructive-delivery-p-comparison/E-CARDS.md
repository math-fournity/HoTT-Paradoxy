# HMZ-018：constructive delivery antecedent cards

## HMZ-E-005 — Constructive existence as verified delivery

```text
source:
  HMZ-S-031 / Palmgren 2004, p. 1.
input:
  a constructive existence proof.
operation:
  extract a program that finds/constructs the purported object.
observation:
  verify the program terminates and is correct.
Done:
  a verified construction is delivered.
bridge type:
  EXPLICIT_FOUNDATIONAL_CONTRACT, not a metaphor.
what it controls:
  existence and usable delivery can be deliberately distinguished in established
  foundational work.
what it does not claim:
  ZFC's axioms promise this Done; an arbitrary proof assistant run supplies it;
  or a user-P preemptive-use cycle exists.
```
