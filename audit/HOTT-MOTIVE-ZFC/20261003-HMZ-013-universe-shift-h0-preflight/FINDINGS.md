This source pair is close to the requested `H0→Z0` route in vocabulary, but it fails the required transport in structure.

Voevodsky gives a genuine technical link from type-theory universes to set-theoretic universes. Shulman gives a genuine actual-consumer issue when changing Grothendieck universes: an object satisfying a property relative to one small universe is not automatically the same object relative to another, and the source instructs the reader to take care. They do not, however, preserve HoTT H0’s subject, process, observation or Done. The source-side ZFC structure is a model/large-cardinal extension with explicit scope changes; the consumer keeps its payment visible; no same-object reentry or pending formation lifecycle occurs.
