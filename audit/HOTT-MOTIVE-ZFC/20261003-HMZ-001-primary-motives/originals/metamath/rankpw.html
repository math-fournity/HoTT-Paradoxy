Theorem rankpw 9849
Description: The rank of the powerset is the successor of the rank. Part of Exercise 30 of [Enderton] p. 207. (Contributed by NM, 22-Nov-2003.) (Revised by Mario Carneiro, 17-Nov-2014.)

Ref Expression

Assertion
Ref Expression
rankpw ⊢ (rank‘𝒫 𝐴) = suc (rank‘𝐴)
